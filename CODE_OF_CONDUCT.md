# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Community standards and enforcement protocols for the **Carbon Engine** project.

## 1. Our Pledge

We pledge to provide an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## 2. Community Standards

| Category | Expected Behaviors | Unacceptable Behaviors |
|---|---|---|
| **Communication** | Empathy, constructive feedback, respect for different viewpoints | Insults, derogatory remarks, trolling, political attacks |
| **Professionalism** | Focus on community well-being, graceful acceptance of critiques | Harassment, public or private intimidation, deliberate disruption |
| **Boundaries & Privacy**| Respecting personal boundaries and shared project scope | Publishing others' private info (doxxing), unsolicited sexual advances |
| **Accountability** | Taking ownership of mistakes, making amends promptly | Defensiveness, repeating harmful conduct, evading moderation |

## 3. Enforcement Guidelines

Project leaders follow a four-tier framework to evaluate and address conduct issues:

| Tier | Severity & Impact | Response & Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Correction** | Inappropriate language or unprofessional behavior | Written private warning with explanation; public apology may be requested |
| **2. Warning** | Single serious violation or continued minor infractions | Formal warning with restricted interactions across project channels |
| **3. Temporary Ban** | Sustained inappropriate behavior or pattern of harm | Temporary suspension from all project spaces for a defined duration |
| **4. Permanent Ban** | Systematic harassment or severe violation of standards | Permanent exclusion from all community spaces and project repositories |

## 4. Reporting & Scope

* **Reporting Channel**: Send private incident reports to `carbon.esgreports@gmail.com` or contact project maintainers directly.
* **Confidentiality**: All incident reports are handled confidentially with the safety and privacy of the reporter prioritized.
* **Scope**: Applies to all project spaces (issues, discussions, PRs, chat rooms) and official external representation of the project.
