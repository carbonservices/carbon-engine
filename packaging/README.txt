================================================================================
Carbon ESG - Enterprise Statutory BRSR Reporting Application
Carbon Services (https://carbonesg.online)
================================================================================

Carbon ESG is an on-device, fully air-gapped desktop platform and compliance
engine for statutory ESG analytics and SEBI Business Responsibility and 
Sustainability Reporting (BRSR).

Key Highlights:
- 100% Air-Gapped & Private: All document ingestion, OCR, and AI analytics 
  execute strictly on your local machine. Zero telemetry, zero data egress.
- Built-In AI Engine: High-performance local inference powered by Gemma 2 2B
  and BGE Small embedding models.
- On-Demand Model Download: Required AI models (~1.78 GB total) can be 
  downloaded and verified directly through the desktop UI or system tray.
- Background Processing: The Carbon Engine daemon runs locally to handle
  document analysis and report generation asynchronously.

System Requirements:
- Operating System: Windows 10/11 (64-bit) or Ubuntu Linux 22.04+ (64-bit)
- Memory: 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended)
- Disk Space: 5 GB free storage (application, AI models, and local storage)
